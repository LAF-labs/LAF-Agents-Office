# LAF Agents Office Design

The interface should feel like a focused operating system for founders, not a developer console. Prioritize dense but calm layouts, direct controls, clear state, and visible evidence for every AI action.

## UX Principles

- Workspace-first: every screen belongs to the current company workspace.
- Operator control: approvals, decisions, members, permissions, memory, and artifacts stay visible.
- Transparent AI: show what ran, what changed, what was remembered, and what needs human input.
- No device setup: do not introduce pairing, terminal commands, or runtime diagnostics in user-facing flows.
- Notion-like collaboration: workspace, members, pages, records, and settings should feel familiar to non-technical founders.

## Core Surfaces

- Home command center
- Startup Office
- Growth center
- Skills
- Wiki memory
- Requests
- Projects and tasks
- Assets and receipts
- Settings

## Visual Direction

Use restrained contrast, 8px-or-less cards where cards are necessary, clear table/list density, and stable dimensions for repeated controls. Avoid marketing hero layouts inside the app. Buttons should use familiar icons for common actions where available.
